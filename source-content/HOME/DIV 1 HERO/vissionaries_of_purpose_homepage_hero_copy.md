# Vissionaries of Purpose Home Page Hero Copy

## Final Hero Copy

### Main Headline

**Where Purpose Meets the Stage.**

### Supporting Text

Artist management, bookings, live music and professional event production, all under one vision.

### Call-to-Action Buttons

- **Primary Button:** Explore Our Artists
- **Secondary Button:** Book Our Services

---

## Hero Copy Discussion

### 1. Main Headline

**Where Purpose Meets the Stage.**

This headline is short, bold and easy to understand. It connects the brand name, Vissionaries of Purpose, with the live music and event-production side of the business.

The word **Purpose** represents the artists, the vision of the company and the meaning behind the music.

The word **Stage** represents live performances, bookings, lighting, sound, backline, LED screens and event production.

The headline works well for the hero section because it is memorable and leaves enough visual space for the bold typographic background.

### 2. Supporting Text

**Artist management, bookings, live music and professional event production, all under one vision.**

This sentence quickly explains the main services offered by Vissionaries of Purpose.

It communicates that the company is more than a record label. It also manages artists, handles bookings and provides professional production services for live events.

The wording is simple so that a visitor can understand the business within a few seconds.

---

## Button Strategy

### Primary Button: Explore Our Artists

**Purpose:** Direct visitors to the Artists page.

This should be the main button because the artists are the public face of the label. It gives visitors an immediate way to discover the talent represented by Vissionaries of Purpose.

**Recommended link:**

`/artists`

**Recommended visual treatment:**

- Solid red background
- White bold text
- Strong contrast
- Slight hover lift or glow
- Larger visual weight than the secondary button

### Secondary Button: Book Our Services

**Purpose:** Direct potential clients to the bookings or services enquiry section.

This button supports sales by helping event organisers, churches, promoters and businesses enquire about artists, live bands, sound, lighting, staging, backline and LED-screen services.

**Recommended link options:**

`/bookings`

or

`/contact`

**Recommended visual treatment:**

- Transparent or black background
- Red or white border
- White text
- Red background on hover
- Slightly less visual weight than the primary button

## Accessibility Notes

- Use real text instead of placing the hero copy inside the background image.
- Maintain strong contrast between the text and the red-and-black background.
- Use clear button labels that explain the destination or action.
- Add visible keyboard focus states to both buttons.
- Keep the headline as the page's main `H1`.
