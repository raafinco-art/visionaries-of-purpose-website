# My Hope Conference 2026

## Event Overview

**Shekinah Christian Worship Centre** presents **My Hope Conference 2026**, a faith-centred gathering focused on the hope, truth and light found in Jesus Christ.

The conference will feature **Minister Tshepiso SK** as the guest artist. Her ministry will form part of a day of worship, teaching, fellowship and spiritual renewal.

## Event Details

- **Event:** My Hope Conference 2026
- **Presented by:** Shekinah Christian Worship Centre
- **Date:** 9 August 2026
- **Morning Session:** 08:00
- **Evening Session:** 17:30
- **Venue:** Shekinah Christian Worship Centre, Tubatse
- **Guest Artist:** Minister Tshepiso SK

## Promotional Copy

**Hope is not simply something we wait for. Hope has a name, and that name is Jesus.**

Join Shekinah Christian Worship Centre for **My Hope Conference 2026**, a powerful gathering created to direct hearts back to the true and lasting light of Christ.

On **9 August 2026**, believers will gather in Tubatse for a full day of worship, ministry and spiritual renewal. The conference will include both a morning and an evening session, creating space for the Word, prayer, fellowship and worship.

As the featured guest artist, **Minister Tshepiso SK** will bring her worship ministry into an atmosphere prepared for encounter. Her presence at the conference will contribute to a gathering centred on faith, restoration and the unchanging hope found in Jesus Christ.

**Registration is online and completely free.**

## Featured Artist Copy

### Minister Tshepiso SK

Minister Tshepiso SK joins **My Hope Conference 2026** as the featured guest artist, carrying a worship ministry rooted in faith, purpose and service.
