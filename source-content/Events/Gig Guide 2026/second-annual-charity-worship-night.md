# Second Annual Charity Worship Night

## Event Overview

Phalane Legacy and AFM City of Refuge present the **Second Annual Charity Worship Night**, a gospel festival created to bring worship, generosity and community impact together in one meaningful gathering.

The evening will feature uplifting worship, live gospel ministry and a shared commitment to helping people in need. Guests are encouraged to attend with clothing, shoes, food donations and a **R50 offering** as part of the event's charitable mission.

## Event Details

- **Date:** 28 August 2026
- **Time:** 18:00 until late
- **Venue:** AFM City of Refuge in Letebejane
- **Event Type:** Gospel Festival
- **Scripture:** Hebrews 13:16
- **Presented by:** Phalane Legacy and AFM City of Refuge

## Featured Artists

- Frank Phalane
- Minister Princess
- Given Mohlala
- Reneilwe
- Simon Kupa

More artists may be announced.

## Entrance

Entrance is based on a charitable contribution. Guests should bring:

- Clothes
- Shoes
- Food
- R50 offering
