### 2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

- **Newsletter information:** your email address, subscription status, the wording or version of the consent you accepted, source page and dates associated with consent, confirmation or unsubscribe actions.
- **Enquiry information:** your name, email address, optional telephone number, enquiry type, selected artist or service, possible event date, message and related consent acknowledgment.
- **Technical and security information:** limited request, device or network information processed by our hosting, database, security and anti-abuse providers to deliver and protect the website. We do not plan to store your raw IP address in the newsletter database by default.
- **Consent preferences:** whether you accepted optional third-party media and the version of the cookie choice shown to you.
- **Third-party platform information:** when you choose to load or follow YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music, ticketing, maps or social-media content, that provider may receive information under its own privacy terms.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information, identity numbers, banking details or confidential health information through the general enquiry form.

### 3. How we collect information

We collect information:

- directly from you when you subscribe, contact us or change a preference;
- automatically where technically necessary to deliver, secure and diagnose the website; and
- from an external platform when you choose to interact with that platform and it provides a response needed for the requested service.

### 4. Why we use information

| Purpose | Information | Proposed basis under POPIA |
|---|---|---|
| Send opted-in news, event announcements and release updates | Email and consent record | Consent, including the direct-marketing requirements in section 69 |
| Confirm, manage or stop a subscription | Email, status and consent event | Consent and compliance with an unsubscribe request or legal duty |
| Answer an enquiry or prepare a requested booking or quote | Contact and enquiry details | Steps requested by you, agreement performance or legitimate operational interests, depending on the enquiry |
| Protect forms and systems from abuse | Limited technical and security signals | Legitimate interests and security obligations, balanced against privacy |
| Keep legally required records and respond to rights or disputes | Relevant records | Legal obligation or legitimate interests in establishing and defending rights |

The final legal-basis descriptions must be checked against the organisation's actual legal status, processes and legal advice. We will not use a newsletter consent to authorise unrelated processing.

### 5. Direct marketing

We send marketing email only where the law permits it and, for the website newsletter, only after you actively select the consent box. The box is not preselected. Each marketing message must identify Visionaries of Purpose and provide a simple way to unsubscribe.

You may withdraw consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in an email or contacting us. Withdrawal does not make processing before withdrawal unlawful. We will update your status promptly and keep only the minimum suppression evidence needed to respect the request and meet applicable obligations.

### 6. Sharing and operators

We may use carefully selected service providers acting as operators or independent responsible parties, depending on their role. Expected categories include:

- Supabase for database and Edge Function infrastructure;
- the confirmed website hosting and content-delivery provider;
- Cloudflare Turnstile for form abuse prevention, if enabled;
- the confirmed email-delivery provider;
- YouTube, Spotify and Apple Music when you activate or follow their media;
- ticketing, calendar, maps or social platforms when you choose their links; and
- professional advisers or authorities when disclosure is legally required.

**OWNER MUST COMPLETE:** confirm the host, email provider, ticketing tools, administrative access roles and all other operators before publication. Sign appropriate operator agreements and configure each provider to collect the minimum information required.

We do not sell personal information.

### 7. Cross-border processing

Some service providers may process or store information outside South Africa. Before using them, we will assess the location, contractual safeguards, privacy protections and applicable requirements for cross-border transfers under POPIA. **OWNER MUST COMPLETE:** record the actual hosting regions and transfer safeguards for Supabase, email delivery, hosting, Cloudflare and any other provider.

### 8. Retention

The following is a proposed schedule and must be approved against operational and legal requirements:

| Record | Proposed retention |
|---|---|
| Active newsletter record | While subscribed |
| Unsubscribe/suppression evidence | Minimum necessary period to respect the opt-out and demonstrate compliance; OWNER MUST SET PERIOD |
| Unconfirmed newsletter request | 30 days, then delete or anonymise unless confirmation occurs |
| General enquiry with no resulting engagement | 12 months after last meaningful contact |
| Booking or contractual enquiry | Contract period plus the legally required accounting, tax or dispute period; OWNER MUST SET PERIOD |
| Security logs | Shortest practical period, proposed 30 to 90 days unless an incident requires longer |
| Consent preference in the browser | Until expiry, withdrawal or replacement by a new consent version |

We delete, anonymise or restrict information when it is no longer required, subject to lawful retention duties and the need to maintain a minimal do-not-contact record.

### 9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information and risk. Planned controls include encrypted HTTPS transport, restricted staff access, Row Level Security on Supabase tables, server-side validation, bot checks, rate limiting, secret management, audit events, backups and dependency updates.

No internet service is completely risk-free. If a security compromise affects personal information, we will investigate and make notifications required by applicable law.

### 10. Your rights

Subject to POPIA and any lawful limitations, you may ask us to:

- confirm whether we hold personal information about you;
- provide access to that information;
- correct or update inaccurate or incomplete information;
- delete or destroy information where the legal requirements are met;
- object to certain processing;
- withdraw consent;
- stop direct marketing; or
- explain a relevant processing practice.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and will not disclose another person's information. Contact the privacy address in section 1 to exercise a right.

You may also lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator. Current complaint channels and forms are available on the [Information Regulator complaints page](https://inforegulator.org.za/complaints/).

### 11. Children

The website and newsletter are not designed to collect personal information directly from children without appropriate authorisation. If you believe a child has submitted information improperly, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action. Event imagery and stories involving children require documented permission and editorial safeguarding before publication.

### 12. External links and embedded services

Our website may link to or, after your choice, load content from third-party services. Their privacy notices and controls apply to their collection. We use local placeholders for optional media so those services do not load merely because you opened a page. See our Cookie Information for more detail.

### 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, suppliers or legal duties change. We will post the revised effective date and, where a change materially affects an existing consent, request a fresh choice rather than treating the old consent as permission for a new purpose.
