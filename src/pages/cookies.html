Pending final review

This notice describes the implemented behaviour, but the production site must still be scanned in a clean browser profile and every provider confirmed before publication. The page is excluded from search indexing until that is done.

Cookies are small text files stored by a website or service. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and identifiers used by embedded services. Some related data may be personal information under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act.

Our default position

  • Necessary website functions may operate without optional consent.
  • No analytics or advertising tracker is used.
  • YouTube, Spotify and Apple media remains a local placeholder until you activate it and the required media choice is present.
  • Rejecting optional media does not block access to page copy, event details, artist information, contact information or external privacy-friendly links.
  • Consent is granular, reversible and versioned.

Your choices

Change your choice at any time using the controls below. You can also reopen them from the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page.

Required to run the site securely, deliver form submissions and remember the choice you make here. These cannot be switched off.

Allows video and music players from YouTube, Spotify and Apple Music to load when you open them. These providers may collect information about your visit under their own privacy terms. With this switched off, you can still follow a link to each platform.

What we store for your choice

We store only the minimum choice data in your browser's local storage. It contains no email address, contact detail or cross-site identifier: 

{
  "version": "cookies-2026-08-05",
  "necessary": true,
  "media": false,
  "updatedAt": "2026-08-05T12:00:00.000Z"
}

The choice expires after about six months, and we request a fresh decision if the purposes or providers materially change.

Third-party media

Before activation, we show a local placeholder, a short explanation and two options:

  • Load this video or music, which records or uses the media choice and then contacts the provider; and
  • Open on YouTube, Spotify or Apple Music, which leaves this website and is governed by that provider.

We do not create a hidden frame, preconnect, DNS prefetch or provider script before the relevant choice. We use the most privacy-protective embed mode each provider offers, but that does not eliminate all processing by the provider.

Form security

Cloudflare Turnstile may process limited technical information to distinguish people from automated abuse. Where practical, it loads only when a protected form becomes active rather than on every page. If the security check cannot be completed, an alternative contact route is available.

Owner must complete

Confirm whether Turnstile is enabled, classify it after the final legal and technical assessment, and publish the alternative contact route.

Changing or clearing a choice

Reopen Cookie settings in the footer at any time. Turning optional media off prevents new third-party embeds from loading and removes players already loaded on the page. A provider you previously loaded may have placed its own storage. You can clear site data through your browser settings and consult that provider's privacy controls.

Browser signals

This site responds to the consent controls described above. We do not currently read automated browser privacy signals, and we do not claim support for a signal the implementation does not read.

Changes and contact
